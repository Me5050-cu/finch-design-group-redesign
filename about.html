About Finch

A family design-build firm, since 2001.

Designers, owners who stay involved, and in-house construction crews — working across Greater Austin since 2001.

Our story

Personal service, at a high standard.

Ted Finch founded the company in 2001 after years in landscape design, wanting a more personal, family-run firm that paired thoughtful design with a high standard of service and craft.

Today Finch works on both residential and commercial properties with dedicated designers, owners who stay involved, and skilled in-house construction and maintenance crews.
