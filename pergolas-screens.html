Azenco R-BLADE™ motorized pergola

Sun, shade or rain. You choose the roof.

Motorized aluminum louvers rotate at the touch of a button. Open them for cool mornings, angle them against the afternoon sun, and close them when a storm rolls in — the built-in gutter carries the water away.

MagnaTrack outdoor screens

Open air when you want it. No bugs when you don’t.

A patented free-floating track uses rare-earth neodymium magnets instead of zippers, so the screen self-corrects as it travels and does not hang up or rewrap.

Pergola questions

Worth it in Central Texas?

Do you charge for the first consultation?

No. Your first on-site consultation is free. We walk the property, talk through goals and budget, and explain how the design process works.

Are louvered pergolas worth it in Central Texas?

For most outdoor living spaces, yes. Motorized louvers give you full shade in July, open sky in October, and rain cover with a built-in gutter — in one aluminum structure that does not need staining.

Are you licensed?

Finch is a licensed irrigator (LI-0030113), a certified Azenco Outdoor and MagnaTrack dealer and installer, and an A+ rated, BBB Accredited business.
